The Challenge

Healthcare was reactive. By the time anyone noticed, it was already too late.

High-risk patients were going home with no meaningful clinical oversight. Nurses were stretched thin. Doctors had no visibility into what was happening between appointments. The only signal that a patient was deteriorating was the patient calling in — or worse, an ambulance arriving.

The COVID-19 crisis made it catastrophically clear: the healthcare system had no infrastructure for proactive monitoring at scale. ICU beds were booked reactively in emergencies. Patients with known risk factors had no daily touchpoint. Critical data from smartwatches and wearables sat completely unused. The system was flying blind.

🏥

Reactive Care Model

Patients only flagged when they called in or collapsed. Early warning signs — gradually dropping SpO2, irregular heart rhythm — went unnoticed for days.

📋

Fragmented Health Records

Medication history, allergies, chronic conditions, and drug interactions were scattered across disconnected systems with no unified clinical view.

⏱️

Delayed Emergency Response

Care teams were notified minutes or hours after a critical event. By then, the window for early intervention had already closed.

🛏️

ICU Bed Crisis

During COVID surges, there was no system to predict bed demand or book in advance. Emergency teams scrambled, and delays cost lives.

⌚

Wearable Data Wasted

Millions of patients wore smartwatches generating rich health data — SpO2, heart rate, HRV — that was never connected to clinical workflows.

💬

No Daily Patient Touchpoint

Patients went days without clinical contact. Deteriorating conditions, missed medications, and mounting anxiety were invisible to their care team.

Why OnPoint Nexus

Other vendors offered dashboards. We built a system that thinks.

When the healthcare network came to us, they'd already evaluated three enterprise vendors. All three proposed the same thing: a data aggregation dashboard that nurses would check manually. The fundamental problem — delayed human response — was being solved with more screens for already-overworked nurses to monitor.

"The system doesn't wait for a nurse to notice. It notices, decides, and acts — in under 30 seconds."

We reframed the problem from day one. The goal wasn't to give nurses more data. The goal was to give the AI more context so it could act before a nurse needed to. That meant connecting four things that had never been connected before: a conversational AI that understood how a patient was actually feeling, Health Gorilla's FHIR API for complete medication and medical history, real-time Kafka streams from smartwatch vitals, and a predictive risk engine trained on all three.

What We Built

Six systems. One platform. One goal — no patient crashes undetected.

Built in parallel across Flutter, React, Node.js, and Python. Every component was designed to work independently and survive partial failures — because in healthcare, uptime isn't a metric, it's a moral obligation.

🤖

AI Nurse Chatbot

Daily conversational monitoring

A conversational AI bot that checks in with patients every day in natural language. It asks how they're feeling, tracks symptom changes over time, flags medication concerns, and feeds every response into the risk engine as structured clinical data.

🩺

Health Gorilla EHR Integration

Complete patient context via FHIR API

Full patient medical history, current medications, known allergies, chronic conditions, and drug interactions pulled in real-time through Health Gorilla's FHIR API. The AI Nurse knows every patient's complete clinical profile before the first conversation.

⌚

Smartwatch Vitals Engine

Real-time streaming via Kafka

Live ingestion of SpO2, heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate, activity levels, and sleep quality from Apple Watch, Fitbit, and Garmin. Kafka handles the stream at scale — thousands of simultaneous patient feeds, zero dropped readings.

🚨

AI Risk Scoring Engine

Predictive ML in Python

A Python ML model that continuously analyzes vitals trends, chat sentiment, medication adherence signals, and historical patterns to produce a real-time risk score per patient. When the score crosses threshold, the alert pipeline fires — automatically.

🛏️

ICU Bed Allocation System

Advance booking before crisis

When a patient's risk score enters the critical range, the system automatically flags bed availability to hospital admin and initiates an advance booking workflow — so when the patient arrives, the bed is ready. No scrambling. No delays.

📱

Dual Platform Architecture

Flutter mobile + React web

Flutter app for patients: daily AI check-ins, medication reminders, vitals history. React web dashboard for care teams: live patient risk board, alert timeline, EHR context, and escalation tools. Both sync in real-time.

AI & Real-Time Architecture

The intelligence layer that connects every signal into a single decision.

The AI isn't a bolt-on feature — it's the central nervous system of the platform. Every message the patient sends, every heartbeat their watch records, every medication flag from Health Gorilla flows through a unified intelligence layer that scores risk and acts autonomously.

🗣️

Conversational Check-ins

Natural language daily conversations that assess symptoms, mood, medication adherence, and pain levels — structured into clinical data automatically.

📊

Predictive Risk Engine

Python ML model analyzing vitals trends, chat responses, and EHR data to predict patient deterioration 4–6 hours before crisis.

⚡

Kafka Alert Pipeline

Real-time streaming architecture delivering tiered alerts — to patient, nurse, and doctor — in under 30 seconds of anomaly detection.

🔬

Vital Anomaly Detection

Pattern recognition on smartwatch streams flagging SpO2 drops below threshold, irregular heart rhythm, abnormal activity, and critical HRV changes.

🧠

Medication Intelligence

NLP parsing of Health Gorilla medication data to surface drug interaction risks, missed dose patterns, and adherence gaps in the care team dashboard.
