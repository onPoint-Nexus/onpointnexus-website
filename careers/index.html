Who we hire

Six traits. If four of them describe you, keep reading.

You question the brief before you accept it. The obvious answer bores you. You look for the one nobody's tried.

You break patterns that need breaking. Not for the sake of it — because the old way is slower, dumber, or both.

45 days isn't a target. It's a promise. You treat every phase like a shipping deadline and take pride in hitting it clean.
