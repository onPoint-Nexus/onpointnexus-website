Our methodology

Six steps between requirement and productive contributor.

Step 01

Requirement Discovery

What you need. In detail.

Our recruitment specialists work closely with the client to understand technical skills, project scope, domain knowledge, team structure, communication expectations, cultural fit, and timeline.

  • Technical skills
  • Project scope
  • Domain knowledge
  • Team structure
  • Communication expectations
  • Cultural fit
  • Timeline
Step 02

Dedicated Talent Sourcing

Six channels. One pipeline.

Our sourcing specialists leverage a combination of internal and external channels to find the right candidates for your requirement.

  • Internal talent pool
  • Global recruitment network
  • Technology communities
  • Professional networks
  • Employee referrals
  • Passive candidate database
Step 03 · Our differentiator

Internal Technical Screening

Every candidate. Every submission.

Every candidate undergoes a comprehensive internal evaluation before being shared with the client. Only candidates meeting our quality benchmarks are submitted for consideration.

  • Technical competency
  • Relevant project experience
  • Domain knowledge
  • Communication skills
  • Problem-solving ability
  • Stability
  • Availability
  • Cultural alignment
Step 04

Interview Coordination

We handle the logistics.

We coordinate scheduling, feedback collection, candidate communication, offer negotiation, and joining confirmation, so your interview panel focuses on evaluation.

  • Interview scheduling
  • Feedback collection
  • Candidate communication
  • Offer negotiation
  • Joining confirmation
Step 05

Verification & Onboarding

Background verified. Day-1 ready.

We coordinate documentation, background verification, compliance, joining formalities, and client onboarding, so your new consultant is fully cleared and productive from day one.

  • Documentation
  • Background verification
  • Compliance
  • Joining formalities
  • Client onboarding
Step 06

Continuous Engagement

The engagement begins after deployment.

Unlike traditional staffing firms, our engagement begins after deployment. Every deployed consultant is supported through structured governance designed to maximise performance, retention, and client satisfaction.

  • Weekly consultant reviews
  • Regular client feedback sessions

  • Resource retention planning
