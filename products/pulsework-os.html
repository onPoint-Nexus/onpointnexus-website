01 · Why Pulse Exists

Five tools stitched together. One workspace instead.

Most mid-size teams end up paying for five different tools that do not talk to each other. Attendance in one place. Leave in another. Projects, KPIs, and people scattered further. Pulse replaces the whole stack.

description

Spreadsheet chaos.

Attendance in one sheet, KPIs in another, appraisals in a doc. Nothing links up. Every quarterly review starts with data archaeology.

warning

Enterprise HRMS overhead.

SAP or Workday means a six-month rollout, a per-seat contract, and workflows built for a 5,000-person company. Overkill for a 40-person team.

block

05 · Frequently Asked

Questions buyers ask before they sign up.

What is PulseWork OS?

PulseWork OS (Pulse for short) is the AI Work OS for mid-size teams. It brings attendance, leave, projects, KPIs and people into one workspace, with an AI assistant that runs your day by voice. Live at pulse-stage.onpointnexus.com.

Who is Pulse built for?

Teams of 20 to 200 people. Especially growing startups that have outgrown spreadsheets but refuse to drown in enterprise HRMS software. Product studios, agencies, professional-services firms, mid-market SaaS teams, and Indian ops-heavy businesses are the sweet spot.

How much does PulseWork OS cost?
  • Pilot. free for up to 50 people, every module included. Available now.
  • Growth. paid tier. Coming soon.
  • Enterprise. custom, on request.
What can the voice AI do?

Ask Pulse by voice to log your day, draft a summary, check who is on leave, or query team KPIs. The voice AI is the fastest interface into the entire workspace. A one-sentence voice log gets structured into the right project, tagged with the right activity type, and rolled into the KPI engine automatically.

How long does setup take?

Under 10 minutes to sign up, set your private subdomain, upload branding, and add your first team. Full rollout to a company typically takes under a day. Each customer gets their own subdomain and can customize the logo and color palette.

What tools does Pulse replace?

Pulse replaces five tools most mid-size teams stitch together: an attendance tracker, a leave manager, a project or task board, a KPI or appraisal spreadsheet, and an org chart or people directory. That's roughly $400/month in SaaS subscriptions for a 50-person team, consolidated into a single workspace with one bill.
