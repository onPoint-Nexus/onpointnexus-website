01 · Why DinePoint Exists

Restaurants in India face three quiet, expensive problems.

description

Menus are static.

Paper menus can't answer questions, can't upsell, can't show what a dish actually looks like, and can't be updated when a price or ingredient changes.

back_hand

Service is invisible.

Guests wave at waiters. Waiters miss tables. Owners don't know how long guests wait until the review lands on Google days later.

campaign

Marketing is manual.

Restaurants beg guests for reviews and Instagram follows. Most guests forget the moment they leave the table.

03 · What's Inside

Four modules. One QR code.

Every module works standalone. All four together turn a restaurant into a connected, measurable operation with a marketing loop that runs on its own.

Diner Experience

  • AI Food Concierge. Natural language chat that recommends dishes by mood, diet, or occasion. Voice input.
  • Multilingual menu. Menu and concierge in English, Hindi, and other Indian languages.
  • Nutrition AI. Auto-generated calories, macros, allergens, and ingredient breakdown per dish.
  • One-tap Call Waiter. Water, bill, cutlery, help. No WhatsApp, no phone numbers.
  • Filters and search. Veg, non-veg, vegan, jain, spice level, price range.

Follow-for-Reward Marketing

  • One-tap follow flow. "🎁 Follow @restaurant → Free dessert today" completed in about ten seconds.
  • Google Review flow. Deep link to the restaurant's review page. Reward unlocked on return.
  • Share a dish. AI caption and branded share card posted directly to Instagram Story.
  • OTP-verified rewards. Phone-number OTP with per-phone daily cap prevents abuse.
  • Configurable offers. Owner sets reward, value, daily cap, and validity from the dashboard.

Waiter & Kitchen Operations

  • Kitchen Display (KDS). Fullscreen TV/tablet view. Green → amber → red as time passes. Audible ding on new alert.
  • Waiter phone app. Web app secured by PIN + name picker. Push, sound, vibration.
  • Live Table Alerts widget. Same feed on the owner's dashboard for oversight and history.
  • Response-time analytics. Diner sees "Avg response 1m 20s today." Proof of good service.
  • No WhatsApp dependency. Works even if the owner is offline. Everything runs inside DinePoint.

Restaurant Dashboard

  • Dish management. Add, edit, reorder, upload photos, mark availability, set tags.
  • QR generator. Download printable QR codes per table or per outlet.
  • Loyalty program. Configure points-per-visit and reward tiers.
  • Weekly reports. Automated summary of visits, top dishes, review score, social growth.
  • Onboarding wizard. Guided setup, restaurant profile, first dishes, first QR in under ten minutes.

07 · Frequently Asked

Questions restaurateurs ask first.

Do diners need to install an app?

No. DinePoint runs entirely in the mobile browser. Guests scan the table QR code and the menu opens instantly. No app store, no login.

What hardware does the restaurant need?

None specifically. The kitchen display runs on any tablet or TV browser, the waiter view runs on any smartphone, and the printable QR codes work on any paper. If you have Wi-Fi and a browser, you have DinePoint.

Does it work in Indian languages?

Yes. Menu and AI concierge both support English, Hindi, and other Indian languages, with voice input on the concierge.

Can we use our own domain for the menu?

Yes. Restaurants can serve their DinePoint menu on their own domain instead of the default dinepoint.in subdomain. Setup is a DNS record and a couple of dashboard clicks.

How does the follow-for-reward marketing work?

After the meal, diners see a one-tap prompt like "Follow us on Instagram to unlock a free dessert." The reward is unlocked via phone OTP with a per-phone daily cap to prevent abuse. Staff verify the code at the table using a PIN-gated screen. Owners configure offers, reward values, and daily caps from the dashboard.

Will it integrate with our POS?

POS and KOT printer integrations are on the near-term roadmap. Today, DinePoint runs alongside your existing POS as a customer-facing and service-alert layer.
