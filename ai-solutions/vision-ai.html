Frequently Asked

Questions buyers ask before they sign.

What kinds of vision problems can you solve?

Image recognition, object detection, food/product identification, defect inspection, medical image analysis, receipt/document OCR, visual search. If humans can do it by looking at an image, a vision model probably can too.

How many languages does the multilingual AI support?

30+ out of the box, including Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, and most European languages. We can add regional dialects with fine-tuning.

Can we train the vision model on our own catalog?

Yes. Fine-tuning on your product catalog or domain-specific images typically takes 500-2000 labeled samples and 1-2 weeks of iteration. Accuracy jumps significantly for domain-specific queries.

Is this fast enough for real-time use?

Yes. Vision inference typically 200-800ms per image. Multilingual chat matches text chat latency. Fast enough for camera-based workflows, live moderation, and interactive assistants.

What are typical use cases?

NutriAI (food identification for nutrition tracking), Sugar Smart Farm Bot (multilingual crop advice with image-based disease detection), quality inspection in manufacturing, multilingual customer support in India and MENA, visual search in e-commerce catalogs.
