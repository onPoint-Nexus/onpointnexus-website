03 · Frequently Asked

Questions buyers ask before they sign.

What is a RAG chatbot?

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is an AI technique where the chatbot retrieves relevant information from your business documents before generating an answer. This grounds the response in your actual content, dramatically reducing hallucination and ensuring brand-accurate replies.

Where is the customer data stored?

Your data stays in a self-hosted vector database that you own. We can deploy on your AWS, Azure, GCP, or on-premise servers. No data is sent to external APIs unless you explicitly choose to use a third-party LLM provider.

What content formats can the chatbot learn from?

Websites (auto-crawled), PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoint files, Markdown, plain text, FAQs, and database content. Content can be added or removed via an admin panel without redeploying the bot.

How long does it take to deploy?

A working prototype is typically ready in 2 weeks. A production-grade deployment with admin panel and integrations is usually 4 to 6 weeks.

Can the chatbot escalate to a human agent?

Yes. When the AI can't answer confidently or detects high-intent queries (sales-qualified leads, complaints, billing issues), it can hand off to your support team via email, Slack, your CRM, or live chat.
