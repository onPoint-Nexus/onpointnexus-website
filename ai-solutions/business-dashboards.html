Frequently Asked

Questions buyers ask before they sign.

What databases do you connect to?

Any SQL database (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, BigQuery, Snowflake, ClickHouse), plus NoSQL (MongoDB, DynamoDB) and warehouses. We also support connecting multiple sources into a single unified view.

Can we get custom charts, or is it templated?

Fully custom. We start from your KPI list, not from a template. If you can describe the chart, we can build it. Common patterns (revenue over time, cohort retention, funnel analysis) are already in our component library and reused.

How does this compare to Tableau or PowerBI cost?

You pay a one-time build cost plus ongoing hosting (typically $50-200/month on your cloud). BI tools bill per seat or per capacity unit. the math flips in your favor after ~15 users or moderate data volume.

Can we control who sees what?

Yes. Role-based access control on views, filters, and even individual metrics. Common patterns: exec dashboard, ops dashboard, per-team dashboards, per-customer dashboards for external sharing.

Do you offer natural-language querying?

Optional. We can add an LLM layer where users type 'show me revenue by region for Q3' and the system generates the SQL and renders the chart. Requires a bit more discovery and validation during scoping.
