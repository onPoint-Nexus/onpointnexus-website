The practice

Government software should be judged by whether an officer, a citizen, and an auditor can all trust the same screen.

Over the last few years OnPoint Nexus has quietly built a portfolio spanning the full width of public-sector digital delivery. From the financial reconciliation of a Gram Panchayat, to municipal property tax, to a statewide public-health platform, to the monitoring of police-housing construction across Maharashtra.

Every one of these systems was built as a working proof of concept, demonstrated to the concerned authority, and taken as far as the engagement allowed. Some are live and in daily use. Others are in active design and pilot conversations.

We build for the whole hierarchy — State, District, Block and Gram Panchayat — with role-based access, clean reporting, local-language interfaces, and an AI layer that turns raw administrative data into decisions.

The AI layer — built in, not bolted on

AI Health Assistant · Marathi

Conversational assistant that answers citizen health questions and guides scheme discovery in the local language.

AI Grievance Management

Auto-categorises and routes complaints, monitors SLAs and drives the escalation matrix.

AI Scheme Recommendation

Matches each family to the government schemes they are actually eligible for, with application guidance.

AI Health Risk Prediction

Flags at-risk citizens and emerging trends so the department can act proactively.

AI Disease Trend Analysis

Turns facility data into surveillance signals and outbreak alerts across districts and talukas.

AI Decision Support

Generates dashboards and narrative reports for officials. The data, already interpreted.
